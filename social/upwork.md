# Upwork — copy and paste

Written for: clients scanning a search results page, deciding in ~3 seconds
whether to open your profile. The title and the first line of the overview
are the entire pitch.

---

## Title (70 char max)

Clients search by outcome, not by framework. Pick one:

**A — AI-led, matches where the money is right now:**
```
AI Platform Engineer | LLM Apps, Next.js & Python | Cloud & Kubernetes
```

**B — broader full-stack net:**
```
Full-Stack Engineer | Next.js, Python & AI Integrations | AWS & K8s
```

Avoid "Web Developer" — it competes with 50,000 profiles on price alone.

## Specialized profiles

Upwork lets you run up to two alongside the main one. Use them:

- **"AI & LLM Application Development"** — highest rates, least competition
- **"Full-Stack Web Development"** — the volume channel

## Rate

Set it deliberately, not defensively. With a co-founded live platform and
Kubernetes in production, you are not a $15/hr profile — pricing yourself
there actively signals junior and filters out the clients worth having.
