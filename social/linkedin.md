# LinkedIn — copy and paste

Written for: recruiters, hiring managers and prospective clients skimming on a phone.
Nobody reads a LinkedIn profile top to bottom. The headline and the first two
lines of About are ~90% of what gets read, so they carry the whole message.

---

## Headline (220 char max)

Pick one. A is the safest for recruiter search, B is stronger for clients.

**A — keyword-dense, good for recruiter search:**
```
Software Engineer | AI Platforms & LLM Orchestration | Next.js · Python · Kubernetes · AWS | Co-Founder @ RenderForge
```

**B — human, stronger for inbound clients:**
```
I build AI-native platforms and the cloud they run on | Software Engineer @ Metafore | Co-Founder @ RenderForge
```

Why not "Full-Stack Developer | React | Node": every second profile says that.
"AI-native platforms" plus "Co-Founder" is what separates you from the pile.
